# VISION.md

# Why Chess Wrapped Exists

Chess players already have statistics.

Chess.com provides ratings.

Win rates.

Game history.

Achievements.

There are already excellent tools for analyzing chess.

Chess Wrapped is not trying to replace them.

---

## A Different Goal

Chess Wrapped was created with a different idea.

Instead of helping players analyze their games,

it helps them celebrate their journey.

A rating is just a number.

A memory is something different.

Chess Wrapped tries to turn statistics into memories.

---

## Story Instead of Analytics

Most chess tools answer questions like:

* How many games did I play?
* What is my rating?
* What opening do I play most?

Chess Wrapped asks a different question.

> "How did this journey feel?"

That small difference changes everything.

---

## Built Around Emotion

This project intentionally avoids becoming an analytics dashboard.

Instead,

it focuses on:

* storytelling
* pacing
* motion
* atmosphere
* delight

The experience is designed to last only a few seconds,

but hopefully remain memorable long after it ends.

---

## Why Design Matters

Good design is not decoration.

Good design helps people feel something.

Every animation,

every illustration,

every transition,

and every empty space

exists for a reason.

Not to impress.

But to make the experience enjoyable.

---

## Why It Is Simple

Many products become more valuable by adding features.

Chess Wrapped becomes more valuable by removing them.

Only the moments that create emotion remain.

Everything else is intentionally left out.

---

## Cute, But Not Childish

Chess is often presented with dark colors,

wood textures,

and traditional styling.

Chess Wrapped chooses a different direction.

Soft colors.

Rounded illustrations.

Gentle motion.

Warm typography.

A calm atmosphere.

The goal is not to modernize chess.

The goal is to make players smile.

---

## Built for Sharing

The final poster is more than an export.

It is the ending of the story.

Something players can keep.

Something they can share.

Something that quietly says,

"This was my chess journey."

---

## A Personal Project

Chess Wrapped started from a simple idea.

"What if chess statistics felt like Spotify Wrapped?"

That idea slowly became something larger.

Not a dashboard.

Not a portfolio piece.

A product.

Something people might genuinely enjoy using.

---

## Design Before Features

Every new idea must answer one question:

> Does this make the experience better?

Not:

> Does this add another feature?

If the answer is no,

it does not belong in Chess Wrapped.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Today,

Chess Wrapped is a simple recap.

Tomorrow,

it may grow.

More scenes.

Better animations.

Richer insights.

New ways to celebrate a player's journey.

But one thing should never change.

The feeling.

The goal is not to build the biggest chess statistics website.

The goal is to build the most memorable one.

---

## One Sentence

Chess Wrapped turns chess statistics into a story worth remembering.
